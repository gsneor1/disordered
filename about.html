An evolution of our first prototype, Disordered is a mobile mindfulness experience designed to promote emotional regulation by allowing users to engage in a calming guided mindfulness journey accompanied by soundscapes and reactive visuals.

Our website implements the research we gathered from the Fall 2024 semester by honing in on key emotions commonly found in different mental disorder groups, and uses further research to provide multiple, effective experiences varying in length. This takes a different approach to our same end goal of promoting mindfulness and wellbeing, and helps users create a temporary safe space to process and feel their emotions, all from whatever device they may have accessible.
